Why Bitcoin Cash? The facts in the story

  • Fee under $0.01 for a typical transaction.
  • Settles in seconds — 0-conf payments between wallets are effectively instant.
  • No accounts. No sign-ups. Works the same everywhere, 24/7.
  • 21 million, never more — fixed supply.
  • CashTokens (2023) — fungible tokens and NFTs built into the protocol.
  • Scales on-chain — adaptive block size (ABLA, 2024) grows with real demand.
