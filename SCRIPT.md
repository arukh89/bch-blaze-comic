# "Kirim Uang Pulang" — a 6-panel comic for BCH BLAZE 2026, Media Track

Original story, written for this hackathon. English captions (the Media
Track judge reads English). All facts checked against bitcoincash.org:
fee under $0.01, settles in seconds, no bank account or sign-up needed,
21M fixed supply, CashTokens (2023), adaptive block size / ABLA (2024).

## Characters (kept consistent across panels)
- **Maya** — Indonesian woman, late 20s, long black hair tied back, teal
  work uniform (she works in Singapore).
- **Ibu Sari** — Maya's mother, 60s, grey-streaked hair in a bun, batik
  blouse, lives in a village in Indonesia.

## Panels

### 1 — Payday in Singapore
Art: Maya finishing her shift, Singapore skyline at dusk behind her,
holding her phone, smiling.
Caption: "Payday in Singapore. Maya wants to send money home to her
mother in Indonesia — like she does every month."

### 2 — The old way
Art: crowded money-transfer counter, long queue, a wall clock, people
waiting with paper forms; Maya frustrated.
Caption: "The old way: a $15 fee, three days of waiting, and a form
for everything. $15 is a week's groceries back home."

### 3 — A friend shows her another way
Art: a friend holds up a phone showing a simple green coin symbol;
Maya leans in, curious.
Caption: "'Try Bitcoin Cash,' her friend says. 'It's cash for the
internet. No bank, no forms — just send it.'"

### 4 — Scan. Tap. Sent.
Art: Maya scanning a QR code with her phone at a hawker stall; a
green checkmark and small coins flying from the phone.
Caption: "Scan. Tap. Sent. The network fee is less than one cent —
and it lands in seconds, not days."

### 5 — Home, seconds later
Art: Ibu Sari in her village house, banana trees outside the window,
phone lighting up her smiling face.
Caption: "2,800 km away, her mother's phone lights up. The money is
already there. No bank account was ever needed."

### 6 — Dinner is on Maya
Art: Ibu Sari buying vegetables at a village market, paying the
vendor with her phone; both smiling; warm sunset.
Caption: "That night, dinner is on Maya. Bitcoin Cash — peer-to-peer
electronic cash. Fee under $0.01 · settles in seconds · 21 million,
never more · tokens built in (CashTokens, 2023)."
